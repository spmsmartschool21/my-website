About SPM Smart School

Learn about our heritage, core values, and vision that drive academic excellence at Sunaprova Phukan Memorial Smart School.

Our Legacy & History

Founded with a deep commitment to educational growth, Sunaprova Phukan Memorial (SPM) Smart School was born out of a vision to provide high-quality English medium primary education for students in Merapani, Bijaypur, and neighboring areas of Golaghat, Assam.

Through a learner-centric approach, we foster an environment that enables students to think independently, explore creative avenues, and excel in both academics and co-curricular activities during their foundational years.

"Our goal is to prepare students not just for examinations, but for life. We aim to equip them with character, confidence, and cognitive abilities to make a positive impact in the world."

Our Vision

To be a pioneering center of primary learning that nurtures creativity, intellectual curiosity, and moral integrity. We envision an educational community that empowers young students from all backgrounds to develop into responsible, confident individuals.

Our Mission

• To provide a rigorous academic program emphasizing experiential learning and foundational literacy.
• To offer an inclusive, safe, and modern smart-class environment for balanced growth.
• To encourage civic responsibility, environmental awareness, and value-based leadership from an early age.
