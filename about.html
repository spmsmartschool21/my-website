Who We Are

Learn about our heritage, mission, vision, and core values at SPM Smart School.

Our Legacy & History

Founded in Merapani, Bijaypur, Sunaprova Phukan Memorial (SPM) Smart School was born out of a vision to democratize quality English-medium primary education for students in rural and surrounding areas of Golaghat, Assam.

Through a learner-centric approach, we foster an environment that enables students to think independently, explore creative avenues, and excel in sports and academic competitions.

"Our goal is to prepare students not just for examinations, but for life. We aim to equip them with character, confidence, and cognitive abilities to make a positive impact in the world."

Our Vision

To be a pioneering center of learning that nurtures creativity, intellectual curiosity, and moral integrity. We envision an educational community that empowers students from all backgrounds to develop into responsible global citizens capable of bringing positive social change.

Our Mission

  • To provide rigorous academic programs emphasizing experiential learning and foundational literacy.
  • To offer inclusive, safe, and state-of-the-art infrastructure for balanced student growth.
  • To encourage civic responsibility, environmental awareness, and value-based leadership from an early age.

Our Core Values

🛡️ Integrity

Fostering honesty, moral principles, and truthfulness in every student.

⭐ Excellence

Encouraging academic rigor and high standards in all aspects of student growth.

🤝 Compassion

Teaching empathy, kindness, and respect towards others and the environment.

💡 Innovation

Cultivating critical thinking, creativity, and adaptability to modern learning tools.
