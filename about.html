About Our Institution

Sunaprova Phukan Memorial (SPM) Smart School

Our Vision & Mission

Established in 2021 at Merapani :: Bijaypur, Sunaprova Phukan Memorial (SPM) Smart School is dedicated to nurturing young minds by blending robust academic foundations with core human and moral values.

Our mission is to foster a dynamic, inclusive learning environment where primary and middle school students develop analytical clarity, digital literacy, confidence, and compassion.

The Institutional "5H" Value Framework

We structure our student development model around five core pillars to ensure balanced physical, mental, and ethical growth:

Head

Intellectual rigor & critical thinking

Heart

Empathy, ethics & emotional intelligence

Hand

Practical skills & creative expression

Health

Physical fitness & mental well-being

Harmony

Social responsibility & community bonding
