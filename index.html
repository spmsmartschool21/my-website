Welcome to SPM

A Beacon of Educational Growth

Sunaprova Phukan Memorial (SPM) Smart School stands as a premier learning institution in the Merapani, Bijaypur region. We offer a comprehensive educational experience designed to nurture young minds from their early foundational years up to Class V.

Our campus provides a safe, disciplined, and stimulating environment designed to inspire curiosity, instill ethical values, and foster core academic skills.
