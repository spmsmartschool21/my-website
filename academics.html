Academics

Discover our holistic academic structure, expert methodologies, and rigorous curriculum designed to prepare young learners for a bright future.

Our Educational Approach

At SPM Smart School, we aim to maintain a high academic standard. Our methodology is rooted in active, experiential learning, utilizing modern technology, and ensuring strong moral grounding. We promote a culture of self-motivation, critical reasoning, and scientific inquiry.

💻

Smart Classrooms

Interactive learning spaces equipped with digital aids to make concepts visual and engaging for young students.

📊

Continuous Assessment

Regular evaluations and personalized tracking ensure every student receives targeted academic support.

🌱

Experiential Pedagogy

Learning through doing—activity corners, practical exercises, and interactive project work.

Academic Divisions & Curriculum

Pre-Primary Education (Nursery - UKG)

Our Pre-Primary program focuses on the cognitive, physical, social, and emotional development of early learners. We use play-way and Montessori-inspired methods to make learning fun and engaging.

Key Highlights:
  • ✔ Theme-based classrooms
  • ✔ Interactive multi-media learning
  • ✔ Low teacher-student ratio for personal attention
  • ✔ Regular parental feedback sessions
Curriculum & Subjects
Literacy & Phonics Numeracy & Shapes General Awareness Art & Craft Physical Play & Motor Skills

Primary School (Classes I - V)

In the primary grades, we transition into more structured learning while keeping student curiosity alive. We cultivate critical reading, mathematical aptitude, and communication skills.

Key Highlights:
  • ✔ Experiential learning projects
  • ✔ Spoken English transition support
  • ✔ Remedial assistance programs
  • ✔ Foundational computer education
Curriculum & Subjects
English Mathematics Environmental Studies / Science Hindi / Assamese Social Sciences (Class V) Computer Science
